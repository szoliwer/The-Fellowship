**Assistant:** Evidence suggests open comment sections get used rarely unless something structures participation. Things that help include invited reviewers who publish their reports openly, community review groups that pick preprints to evaluate, short structured forms rather than free text, and author responses displayed alongside comments. Visibility also matters; if comments are surfaced next to the preprint and linked from the published version, authors have more incentive to engage.
